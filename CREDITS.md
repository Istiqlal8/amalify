# Credits

## Murottal player backdrops (`assets/videos/`)

Carried over from the earlier Quran app. Each clip was trimmed to 8 seconds, cropped to
portrait, graded slightly darker and played forwards-then-backwards so the loop has no
seam. Audio was removed. The CC BY licences require the author to be named wherever the
clip is used. The credits are published at https://amalify.my.id/credits.html (`docs/credits.html`),
and the Murottal list screen links to that page.
